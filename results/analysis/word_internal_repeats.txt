Reading: the lag-5 bigram clustering is significant (P ~ 1e-4 before the look-elsewhere factor) and word-internal. A per-word period-5 key reproduces the clustering but, if aligned in more than ~25% of the pairs, predicts a lag-5 difference histogram that LP2 does not have; an independent copy process does not reproduce the clustering. No mechanism is identified. The finding is a constraint for any model of the key: whatever it is, it repeats runes at distance five inside words and not across word boundaries.
