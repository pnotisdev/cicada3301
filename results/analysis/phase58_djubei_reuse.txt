== 4. constraint ==
DJU BEI is the same 6 ciphertext runes twice. For c' = c + s*k (mod 29) the plaintexts are equal iff k[a+t] = k[b+t], t = 0..5, a, b the key positions of the copies. With no skipped or inserted runes b - a = 6395; otherwise b - a = 6395 plus the extra key steps in between (the scan samples 5700-6500). Equal runs survive any sign or multiplier. So any screen that reports a key family and offset must also show k[a..a+5] = k[b..b+5] there; a periodic family passes only if its period divides b - a, and a non-periodic one only by chance (29^-6 = 1.7e-9 per pair). If the plaintexts differ the difference k[a+t] - k[b+t] is unconstrained, so only the equal-plaintext reading gives a test.
