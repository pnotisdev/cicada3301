C. THE 5x6 BIFID AS A MAP
   A Bifid needs every re-paired coordinate to be a cell. In a 5 x 6 square a column index 6 can land where a row is read
   (or a row index 6 where a column is read in 6 x 5), and the 30th cell is empty: the map is not a bijection.
   rune squares: 958 of 960 English windows of 261 symbols cannot be encrypted at all (8 squares x 12 periods x 10)
   letter squares: 480 of 960 English windows of 261 symbols cannot be encrypted at all (8 squares x 12 periods x 10)
   Decrypting page 12 itself with the rune squares (strict) leaves 3.1-10.7% of its positions without a plaintext symbol (mean 7.3%).
   Claimed sentence in runes: a 5x6 Bifid decryption can output it in 22539 of 98880 placements (8 squares x periods 0, 2-60 x every offset in a 261-symbol page; 285 square/period pairs). The other placements need a pair that is not a cell.
   Claimed sentence in letters: a 5x6 Bifid decryption can output it in 67862 of 131040 placements (8 squares x periods 0, 2-60 x every offset in a 331-symbol page; 287 square/period pairs). The other placements need a pair that is not a cell.
