# Phase 65: prime clause sums as a language-free score (2026-10-23)

Nothing in LP2 is solved. Script `work/phase65_prime_clause_score.py` -> `results/analysis/phase65_prime_clause_score.txt`.

## 1. Plan (written before running)

- Score: a unit (the runes between two clause marks, '.' or '&' as in phase 64) is "prime" when the sum of its runes' Gematria prime values (`lp.PRIME`: F = 2, U = 3, ..., 109) is prime. Score of a decryption = number of prime units among the units whose marks are known from the ciphertext layout.
- (1) Calibration on the solved plaintext (LP1, segments 0-6, decrypted with `lp.KNOWN`): share of prime units for LP1's own clauses, for merged units (2, 3 clauses; greedy until >= 77 runes, LP2's sentence size) and per paragraph. Baselines at the same unit lengths: uniform random runes (what a wrong key gives), the LP1 plaintext's own runes shuffled across units (parity / frequency bias kept), and consecutive English prose runes (phase 22/25 corpora, spelled as in phase 25).
- (2) Power: LP2's actual unit lengths per section and for n300 windows from each section start; wrong-key null = uniform runes with the unit's length (probability b per unit by simulation), true model = each unit prime with probability p (LP1's own rate, and weaker). Exact Poisson-binomial tails; detection threshold at alpha 1e-3, 1e-6, 1e-9; units needed for 80% power; combined with the IoC separation (d' of independent scores adds in quadrature).
- (3) Rescoring of kept hits: every `decoded :` line of the `*verify*` files in `results/` (the first 160 runes of each re-decoded hit at a section window; Latin spelling is ambiguous: planned an optimistic parse, replaced by a greedy parse, see results) against the same procedure applied to random rune strings (matched null).
- (4) One paragraph on phase 64's observation (clause-final words not longer than average), the explanations it allows, and a cheap keyless check.
- Light CPU, one thread, below-normal priority.

## 2. Results (`results/analysis/phase65_prime_clause_score.txt`; 18 CPU-s; an exact-parse search for the rescoring was dropped after 24 CPU-min wasted at 3 s per string, a greedy parse with the same procedure on the null replaced it)

**(1) Calibration.** LP1 decrypted, units cut at '.' and '&': **39 of 88 clauses (0.443) have a prime Gematria sum**; uniform random runes at the same lengths give 0.142 (sd 0.036), the LP1 plaintext's runes shuffled across the same lengths 0.145 (P 0.0005), English prose runes at the same lengths 0.146 (parity bias included: the baseline is not 0.5-based).
The trace sits at the clause level only: 2 clauses merged 0.178 (baseline 0.125, P 0.21), 3 merged 0.125, merged to >= 77 runes (LP2's sentence size) 0.121 (baseline 0.118, P 0.57), paragraphs 0.286 of 7 (P 0.17). So sentence-sized units built from LP1 carry no prime-sum signal at all: the authoring is per comma-sized clause (phase 64: LP1's marks are comma-sized).

**(2) Power.** LP2's 167 units (mean 77.5 runes; wrong-key prime probability b = 0.129) give per section: 9-43 units. Critical count at alpha 1e-6: 8 of 9 (0.7, 0.12) ... 19 of 43 (0.15). If LP2 were authored at its own '.' level at LP1's rate p = 0.44, the power at alpha 1e-6 is 0.01 (0.7, 0.12), 0.07-0.16 (0.8-0.14) and 0.57 for 0.15 only; for p = 0.30 or 0.20 it is 0.00-0.03. **Units needed for 80% power**: p = 0.44: 29 / 56 / 82 units at alpha 1e-3 / 1e-6 / 1e-9; p = 0.30: 84 / 165 / 242; p = 0.20: 407 / 816 / 1,217. LP2 has 167 units in total and at most 43 in a section.
An n300 window holds 2.9 complete units on average: power 0.000 at alpha 1e-6. Against the IoC (English prose n = 300: 1.78 vs 1.00 random, sd 0.024, d' = 32.4) the prime score adds d' = 1.6 for an average window (combined 32.4): it is no help in a campaign where English plaintext shows in the IoC / bigram score; its only use is language-free confirmation of a candidate whole section (0.15 with p = 0.44, or the sum over several sections).

**(3) Rescoring.** 1,820 distinct (section, decoded string) records from the `*verify*` files (108 lines without an LP2 window dropped); the first 160 runes at the section start hold 1-3 complete units; Latin spelling parsed greedily, the null goes through the same spelling and parse. Prime fraction 0.142 (records) vs 0.141 (matched random decodes); records with all units prime and >= 2 units: 18 of 1,399 (1.29%) against 0.95% in the null (z about +1.3, P about 0.12). Best: section 0.8, 3 of 3 units (phase 50 cross_C nulledge2 verify); none distinguishes itself, and no record is a candidate by IoC. Nothing to report; the score has no power at 1-3 units.

**(4) Phase 64's clause-final observation.** Clause-final word minus mean word: LP1 +1.49 (se 0.23), LP2 -0.25 (se 0.18, 167 clauses); first word LP1 -0.16, LP2 -0.05. So LP2's final words are 1.4 se below zero, not "shorter" in a significant sense: the finding is the missing +1.3 of English. Explanations: (a) LP2's marks do not sit at plaintext clause ends (layout or an independent rule): 0 for first and last words; (b) R4's extra rune avoids final words (the null never written after a word's last rune): about -0.4 for the last word (relative to an English +1.3 this alone would not remove it); (c) the word cuts are not plaintext words: 0; (d) the text is not English-like: 0. -0.25 +- 0.18 does not separate 0 from -0.4.
A cheap keyless check that does separate (b) from (a, c, d): in the R4 simulator (phase 57) place marks at the sentence ends of the English plaintext (so (a), (c), (d) do not apply) and measure the same statistic with the null slot allowed / forbidden after the last rune: (b) predicts a deficit of about -0.4 only when the slot is forbidden, otherwise +1.3 minus the usual dilution; 500 simulations, seconds. Not run here.

## 3. What is NOT excluded
Prime-sum authoring in LP2 at a level other than its '.' units (sub-units not marked on the page, units that cross sections) is not tested: the only calibrated unit is LP1's comma-sized clause; extra runes (R4 nulls and repairs) in a true decryption would change the sums unless removed, so a real LP2 decryption need not show p = 0.44 even if authored; recorded decodes cover 160 runes only; the optimistic parse was not used (greedy spelling parse, matched null).
