# Phase 49: Mike's period 13 (full selection pipeline) and the 1033.jpg blob

Two tasks. Mike's package (`work/ref/user_downloads/lp-checks/`) was read as data (README, `lpchecks.py`, results JSON); none of his code was run. Everything below is re-implemented in `work/p13_full/` and, for task B, `work/blake1033/`.

## Task A: period 13 on 54+55.jpg, with the selection included

### Preregistration A (written before the null/plant runs; the real stream had been run once, see "Disclosure")

**Statistic.** For a stream of 9 sections (LP2 = `lp.py` segments 0.7-0.15, 12,956 runes), split into paragraphs at `&`. Per paragraph with >= 150 runes (11 of them: 729, 693, 333, 1729, 1894, 1021, 1433, 1468, 2474, 534, 308; the 308-rune one is 54+55.jpg) and per period p = 2..40, the delta stream d[i] = c[i]-c[i-1] mod 29 (c[-1] = 0 at the paragraph start); column IoC x29 where column = (index among kept positions) mod p; best over "no skip" and all subsets of up to 5 positions (never position 0) that share one delta value; skipped positions are removed and do not advance the column. S = overall maximum over paragraph x period. Decision: LP2's S against the distribution of S in the nulls; "significant" means the empirical upper tail is below 0.05 (one-sided).

**Search.** Exact exhaustive (all subsets, via a 2-D prefix-table evaluation that I verified against a brute-force enumeration and a direct IoC computation) for paragraphs <= 340 runes (308, 333). Beyond that exhaustive is impossible in the budget (2,474 runes: about 10^9 subsets per period). So: 340 < L <= 1100 (534, 693, 729, 1021): per-delta-value greedy (add the best skip, up to 5, keep the best IoC seen); L > 1100: no-skip IoC only. This is a weaker search than exhaustive for those paragraphs, applied identically to LP2, nulls and plants. Measured greedy-vs-exact deficit on 308-rune random streams: mean 0.043 IoC, max 0.235 (greedy equals exact in 5/20 cases); the gain of the greedy search over no skip is 0.29 (L = 308), 0.16 (729), 0.11 (1021), 0.09 (1729) on random streams, so long paragraphs matter little for the maximum, but they are not exactly covered.

**Nulls.** (1) `perm`: deltas permuted within each section (paragraph lengths kept; each section keeps its multiset of paragraph-start/other deltas), 200 streams. (2) `fresh`: uniform random runes with the 81% anti-repeat rule (a would-be repeat is redrawn with probability 0.81), same paragraph lengths, 200 streams. Seeds are the run index.

**Plants** (a fresh stream whose 308-rune paragraph is replaced): the paragraph = 303 deltas + 5 inserted deltas of one random value at random distinct positions (> 0); the 303 kept deltas = key[col mod 13] + e, rebuilt into ciphertext by cumulative sum. `plant_key` e = 0 and the key offset is kept with probability q (else uniform); q = 0.15 (calibrated so the exact period-13 IoC with skips averages about Mike's 1.715: 1.69 +- 0.20) and q = 0.25 (about 2.8). `plant_eng`: e = real English plaintext deltas (Emerson, mapped to the 29 runes) + a random period-13 offset, q = 1 (a true period-13 Vigenere on English). Detection = S above the 95th percentile of the fresh-null S; also reported: S above the 95th percentile of the perm null, and whether the arg max is (308-rune paragraph, p = 13). 60 / 40 / 40 plants.

**Not tested / limits.** Greedy and no-skip tiers above; one skip value per paragraph is what Mike searched, no free skips; fresh null ignores any non-uniform structure of the real alphabet; plants use deltas directly (a skip is a delta of value v that does not advance the key).

### Disclosure
Before this preregistration was written I ran the pipeline on the real LP2 once while timing it (result (S = 1.9446 at paragraph 308, p = 39; no-skip maximum 1.553)); the rules above were fixed after that only for runtime (the tiers), not by looking at the null distribution.

(Results follow below.)

## Task B: the 1033.jpg blob (MMMCCCI / MXXXIII near Newton's finger)

### Data
`work/ref/blake_1033/` (git-ignored, added to `.gitignore`): Wikimedia original `Newton-WilliamBlake_wikimedia.jpg` from `https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:FilePath/Newton-WilliamBlake.jpg` (resolves to `upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/0/0e/Newton-WilliamBlake.jpg`), 4,702 x 3,617, 1,888,843 bytes, SHA-256 `d70f3d99247ababbe25e8b3111053fbd9e41362b46aca800e4a8b3b174fe34f4` (byte-identical to the archive's copy in the debate folder); `1033.jpg` from the archive (1,327 x 1,427, SHA-256 `15b2b99fe15ff34ef3eab574bb84bfaad0a119674be2ca5baf65951e6a9bd865`); the community's `Outt.png` (cicada logo, 521 x 523 LA) and `P6Bg828.jpg` (their difference image).

### Difference image reproduced (`work/blake1033/align.py`, `diff.py`)
Mirror the Wikimedia original left-right; normalised cross-correlation of a 480 x 420 grey template of 1033.jpg's centre panel against it (FFT): peak NCC 0.9976 at integer offset (216, 2310) of the mirrored original, i.e. the panel is an uncropped-resolution crop, no resize, as the community says. Panel in 1033.jpg = x 296..1031, y 0..1011 (736 x 1012; outside it the median difference is large: other source images). Mean-abs-RGB difference x8 is `work/blake1033/diff_x8.png`: JPEG-grid noise everywhere in the panel, faint ghost lines of the figure, and one compact two-line mark at x about 744-790, y about 950-968 (two lines, the lower one narrower), left of the finger tip. That matches the community's image (`P6Bg828.jpg`).

### Preregistration B (written before any hypothesis is rendered)
Patch: a window around the mark in the panel (luma of the RGB difference I - S, with S = the aligned, mirrored Wikimedia crop). Hypotheses rendered into S at the mark's position: (a) the archive logo `Outt.png`, scaled to the mark (variants: height-fit / width-fit / stretched to the mark's bounding box; normal, mirrored, rotated 180); (b) text MMMCCCI above MXXXIII rotated 180 degrees, in a plain font (Arial / Times / DejaVu where present), line widths matched to the two lines of the mark. Composite with free colour (white/black), opacity (3 values) and +-2 px shift, then JPEG-compress the panel region using the quantisation tables and subsampling read from 1033.jpg (these correspond to the 'about 45%' quality; the quality implied by the tables is reported) and decode. Score = normalised (zero-mean) correlation between (rendered - S) and (real I - S) over the window, maximised over the variants and the free parameters. Null: the identical optimisation with the identical rendering at K = 400 other positions of the panel (offsets from the mark that are multiples of 16 so the JPEG block/MCU phase is the same; at least 64 px from the mark and the panel border), scored against the real I - S there. Report each hypothesis' score, its z and rank against the null, and the difference of the two scores against the null distribution of that difference. A matching hypothesis needs a score outside the null range (rank 1 of 401). Limits: font/size/position freedom is finite (the original editing tool is unknown); the real file was JPEG-compressed after an unknown number of edits, so only the final compression is simulated.

### Results A (all runs: `work/p13_full/out_*.jsonl`, summary `analysis_output.txt`, code `pipe.py`, `run.py`, `selftest.py`, `analyse.py`)
**Controls of the search.** The prefix-table evaluation equals the direct column IoC to 0 on random skip sets (p = 3, 7, 13); the exact search equals a brute-force enumeration of all subsets on 6 random streams (L = 60, p = 2, 5, 8, 13). **Mike's numbers reproduced** by this independent code: no-skip IoC 1.3770 (c[-1] = 0 convention), 1.7155 with the best <= 5 skips of one value, p = 13, 308 runes.

**LP2 against the full pipeline** (11 paragraphs x periods 2-40, ~5 s per stream):

| | S (overall max) | where |
|---|---|---|
| LP2 | **1.9446** | 308-rune paragraph, p = **39** (not 13); no-skip maximum 1.554 |
| perm null (200) | mean 1.792, sd 0.083, q95 1.928, max 2.083 | tail P(S >= LP2) = 7/200 = **0.040** |
| fresh null (200) | mean 1.787, sd 0.084, q95 1.931, max 2.065 | tail P(S >= LP2) = 7/200 = **0.040** |

- The statistic is dominated by the two exact-search paragraphs (308: 61-70% of the null maxima, 333: the rest) at high periods (30-40; about 8 runes per column). The p = 13 cell itself is never the null's argmax (0/400); its null range at (308, p = 13) is mean 1.323, max 1.570 (perm) / 1.518 (fresh); LP2's 1.7155 exceeds all 400.
- **Fixed-cell p (what Mike reported): P = 0.005** (0/200 perm, 0/200 fresh at (308, p = 13), resolution floor 1/201). **After selection over the 78 exactly-searched cells (333 and 308 runes x 39 periods), P(min-p <= LP2's) = 0.26 (perm), 0.26 (fresh)** (`cells.py`, exploratory). Over the whole pipeline (raw max): 0.040. So the selection removes the claim's significance for p = 13 in particular; it leaves a marginal 0.04 for "something in the 308-rune paragraph", located at p = 39.
- **Post hoc, not preregistered** (seen only after S's argmax was p = 39): the lowest-p periods of the 308-rune paragraph are 13, 14, 39, 26 (perm) and 13, 39, 14, 26 (fresh), i.e. all three multiples of 13 up to 40 are in the top four. Per-cell p of (13, 26, 39): 0.005 / 0.020 / 0.015 (perm), 0.005 / 0.030 / 0.005 (fresh). The statistic -sum log p over {13, 26, 39}: LP2 13.4 (perm), 14.1 (fresh), null mean 2.8; 0/200 nulls reach it in either model (floor 1/201). A true period-13 structure would show at its multiples; noise would not especially. This is one statistic chosen after the fact (several were conceivable), so it is a lead, not a result; a clean test would be pre-registered on another stretch (e.g. the 333-rune paragraph: p13 / p26 / p39 cells 1.37 / 1.44 / 1.47, null-compatible, per-cell p(13) = 0.18-0.20).

**Power (planted period 13 in the 308-rune paragraph of a fresh stream, 5 skips of one value):**

| plant | exact IoC at (308, 13), mean | S mean | S above null q95 (fresh / perm) | arg max = (308, 13) | fixed cell (13) above null q95 (1.450) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `plant_key` q = 0.15 (Mike-strength: 1.71) | 1.706 | 1.951 | 28/60 / 28/60 (47%) | 3/60 | 53/60 (88%) |
| `plant_key` q = 0.25 | 2.729 | 2.895 | 40/40 (100%) | 5/40 | 40/40 |
| `plant_eng` (English plaintext deltas + a random period-13 offset, i.e. a real period-13 Vigenere) | 1.371 | 1.773 | 2/40 (5%) / 2/40 | 0/40 | 3/40 (8%) |

So the full-pipeline statistic detects a period-13 key whose strength equals Mike's observed 1.7155 in about 47% of plants (the fixed-cell test 88%, which is what Mike used and what costs the 15%-class selection), and it has **no power (5% = the false-positive rate) against a genuine period-13 Vigenere on English**, because the column IoC of English deltas is low (the plant's exact IoC at p = 13 is only 1.37 against the null mean 1.32). The plant detections also pick the harmonic cells often (arg max rarely at p = 13: 3/60, since 26 and 39 inherit the structure and have higher variance), which makes LP2's arg max at p = 39 unsurprising for a real period-13 effect but equally likely for noise (p = 30-40 are the null's usual maxima).

**Limits:** greedy-per-value search (not exact) for 534-1021-rune paragraphs and no skip search for paragraphs > 1100 runes (greedy deficit vs exact: mean 0.043, max 0.235 IoC on 308-rune random streams); nulls 200 each (tail resolution 1/201); 'perm' keeps each section's delta multiset; plants are built in the delta domain; the plant strength q is calibrated to Mike's number so the detection rate is for that strength only.

The preregistered metric is weak in absolute terms: JPEG quantisation of nearly the same pixels gives a shared noise (no overlay at all: NCC 0.59 with the real difference patch), so many null positions score 0.65-0.9. The *comparison* is robust: at every setting the text beats the logo by far more than any null position does. The baseline-subtracted analysis (added after seeing that; labelled post hoc) removes the shared noise and gives a clean separation: text 0.64 (null max 0.24), logo 0.17 (3/400 null positions reach it).
**Shape control (post hoc, real blob only, same optimisation, baseline-subtracted):** two plain filled rectangles at the two line boxes score **0.644 = the same as the text (0.643)**; scrambled letters (CMCMICM over XMIXIIX) 0.51. So the data show *two horizontal bands of added light pixels with a gap between them* (the layout of two text lines), strongly against a compact cicada-shaped mark; they do not resolve the letters at about 5-6 px line height, so 'MMMCCCI over upside-down MXXXIII' is supported only as far as 'two lines of text-like marks'. The logo hypothesis fits worse (aspect ratio 2.8:1 of the cicada logo matches the 46 x 18 px box, but its solid body fills the gap that the data leave dark). Whether the marks were added in the collage editor or are an editing leftover with the same two-band shape is not decided by pixel evidence; the leftover-logo reading is disfavoured, an added-text reading is favoured.
**Limits:** font, size and placement were fitted to the blob's measured line boxes for both hypotheses (logo: bounding box; text: line widths), single-pass JPEG only (the real chain is unknown), only luma compared, Arial / Times / Arial Narrow and the 4 orientations of the logo.
