# R4 repair s03: does LP2 prefer some two-null words? (2026-10-24)

Nothing in LP2 is solved. Keyless simulation only; no LP2 ciphertext was decrypted, only LP2's nine summary statistics of phase 57 were compared. Idea 6 of `work/laptop_review/11_next_100.md`.

## Question
R4 (phase 57) assumes at most one null per multi-rune word. Does LP2 accept a mixture in which a share f2 of the null-carrying words has two nulls? Can the word-length statistic see it?

## Method
- Script `work/r4fix/s03_null_count.py` (`python -B work/r4fix/s03_null_count.py --reps 500 --workers 2`, below-normal priority, 2 workers). Output `results/r4fix/s03_null_count/summary.txt` (plus `rows.pkl` raw replicates and `run.log`).
- Simulator = a copy of `phase57_mechanism.simulate` (R4 settings: q 0.81, T repair, checked null, not blind) with the null logic generalised: each multi-rune word draws a null with probability `rate` (= r1 + r2); with probability f2 it carries a second null. Each null has its own independent uniform slot (`any` = 0..L, `interior` = 1..L-1; two nulls on the same slot are adjacent); every null is re-drawn if it equals its left neighbour. Measurement (`measure`, the nine statistics, z, combined chi2, P = share of replicates with chi2 >= LP2's) is phase 57's own code, imported read-only. `work/r4fix/r4sim.py` was not used (a per-word python simulator, far slower for 14 settings x 500 replicates).
- Grid f2 in {0, 0.05, 0.10, 0.15, 0.20, 0.30} for slots `any` and `interior`, plus f2 = 0.50 (slots `any` only, an extra point added to bound the range from above). For each f2 the rate is tuned (3 secant steps of 100 replicates, base at rate 0) so the mean word count equals 2,896; achieved means 2,890.7-2,898.5. 500 replicates per setting; seeds 1000+i (setting index i), tuning seeds 7, 20-22. Total nulls per multi-rune word, rate x (1 + f2), is 0.45 across the whole grid (0.449, 0.447, 0.449, 0.447, 0.450, 0.450, 0.449): f2 moves nulls between words, it does not change how many there are.
- Selftest (in the script, passes): at q 0 and rate 1 every multi-rune word has exactly 1 (f2 0) or 2 (f2 1) nulls and no 1-rune word has any; interior slots never sit at a word edge; rate 0 gives no nulls; f2 1 gives fewer words than f2 0 at the same rate; the share of two-null words at f2 0.3 is 0.30 +- 0.03.
- DATA NOTE: a first run was made before the orchestrator copied `work/ref/corpora/*.txt` into this worktree (`fam_data.english` silently fell back to Emerson only). That run did not reproduce phase 57's R4 (control word count 2,864, chi2 8.4) and was DISCARDED; its summary is kept only as `work/tmp/s03_emerson_only/summary_emerson_only.txt`. Everything below is the rerun with the full corpora (18 files).

The other eight statistics hardly move with f2: doublets in-word 59-60 (z +0.4 to +0.5) / across gaps 17.1-17.5 (z +1.4), T in-word 76-79 (z +0.1 to +0.3), best other form 67-69 (z -0.3 to -0.7), lag 2-12 max z +1.9 to +2.0 (the same residual as phase 57), unigram and bigram flat. Full nine-statistic rows for both slot rules are in `summary.txt`. The word-length z rises smoothly with f2 (+0.3 to +4.1): LP2 has more 3-rune words (24.9%) than any grid setting (19-24%), and extra two-null words move mass from 3-rune to 2-rune words (14.4 -> 18.3%), against that excess. LP2's 2-rune share (15.47%) alone matches f2 about 0.10, but the whole histogram (G) does not prefer it.

**Verdict.** LP2 accepts (P > 0.05) every grid value f2 = 0 to 0.30 under both slot rules; f2 = 0.50 is rejected (P 0.010, G z +4.1). The upper edge lies between 0.30 (P 0.26) and 0.50. The minimum chi2 is at f2 = 0 (any slots) and 0 (interior), but chi2 is flat from 0 to 0.20 (any: 6.9-8.4; interior: 5.7-7.5), so LP2 shows no preference: a two-null share up to 0.3 (about 1 in 3 null-carrying words) is not excluded and no value is favoured. Can the word-length statistic distinguish f2? Only coarsely. The mean histogram moves by about 1 percentage point of the 2- and 3-rune bins per 0.15 of f2: G of the mean histogram against the f2 = 0 one is 1.1 / 4.1 / 8.6 / 13.5 / 27.6 / 56.6 at f2 0.05 / 0.10 / 0.15 / 0.20 / 0.30 / 0.50, while one replicate's G against its own pooled histogram has mean 30.5 and sd 15.6 (df 10). One 2,896-word text therefore separates f2 = 0.5 from 0 about half the time and f2 <= 0.15 not at all (recovery control: best f2 scattered, true value accepted 19/20). Practical consequence: a decoder that models 0 or 1 null per word is not contradicted by the histogram; a two-null share of 0-0.3 is an open nuisance parameter, not excluded.

## What this does NOT exclude
f2 up to 0.30 in either slot rule; more than two nulls per word; null counts that depend on word length (phase 38/47: a length-independent +1 fits, per-rune scaling does not); correlated slots (two nulls drawn independently here, not e.g. one at each edge); plaintext other than English digraph spelling; structured keys. A fit of stated simulations (Level 1), not evidence that the mechanism is real.
