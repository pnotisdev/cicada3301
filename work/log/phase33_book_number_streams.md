# Phase 33: Cicada's cited books as NUMBER streams (key computed from the book) (side session, 2026-10-21)

Nothing in LP2 is solved. This closes the phase-22 gap "keys computed from a book (sums, hashes, book-code numbers as numbers)" for one concrete family: numbers derived from the text of six cited books, used as an additive key under the skip / coin anti-repeat rule by the unchanged kscreen engine.

## Streams (`work/kscreen/booknum.py` -> `work/kscreen/data/booknum.ksb`, `booknum.tsv`; git-ignored data)
Books (local copies, data only): Liber AL (OTO text), Blake MHH, Blake Songs, Emerson's Self-Reliance (the essay only), the Mabinogion (Guest), Agrippa 1651 (OCR, noisy). Two spellings each: dg = digraph runes, lt = letter by letter. All values mod 29.
Families: Widx / Wpri (per-word sum of rune indices / of GP prime values), Lpri (per-rune prime value), Plpri / Plidx (prefix sums of the per-rune prime / index), Pwidx / Pwpri (prefix sums of the per-word sums), Sidx / Spri (per-sentence sums; sentence = text up to . ! ? ; :), Wlen / Plen (word lengths / their prefix sums).
Flat-IoC rule as `signed.py` (predicted IoC under English plaintext, both signs, <= max(1.003, 1.002 + 1.6/n)); minimum 400 values; streams over 400,000 values cut at 400,000 (Plpri and Plidx of Mabinogion and Agrippa, 8 streams: only the first 400,000 positions are screened).
**Built 132, kept 80.** Kept: Plen, Plidx, Plpri, Pwidx, Pwpri 12 each (6 books x 2 spellings), Sidx and Spri 10 each (2 of 12 too short: Liber AL has fewer than 400 sentences by this splitter in both spellings). Excluded by the flat-IoC rule: **all 36** of Widx, Wpri, Lpri, Wlen (12 books-spellings each): per-word sums, per-letter prime values and word lengths are not flat mod 29 (as expected: their raw histograms are skewed), only cumulative sums and long sentence sums are. 5,729,596 (stream, offset) pairs for a 300-rune window.

## Positive controls (`work/kscreen/booknum_selftest.py`, results/kscreen/booknum_selftest.txt)
Independent Python planter (imported from `signed_selftest.py`, not the engine's `plant`): 300 runes of held-out English, one plant per kept family with the shortest and the longest stream of the family (14 plants), skip / coin, multiplier 1 / 28, plain / substituted alphabet. **14 of 14 PASS**: the engine returns the stream at the planted offset (Spri agrippa lt at 8993 for a truth of 8991, the skip alias) with best IoC 1.699-1.773 and a re-decode at the truth 1.699-1.768; a stream of another book on the same window stays at 1.229-1.311 (< 1.40). Streams up to 400,000 values were planted (Plidx and Plpri mabinogion lt at offset 266,412).

## Screen (`kscreen screen data/segments.txt file:data/booknum.ksb`, 8 section windows of 300 runes, models skip + coin, multipliers 1 and 28, every offset, 2 threads; 183,347,072 decodes, 2,819 s)
- **Real: best IoC 1.3462** (window 0.15, Plidx of the Mabinogion, digraph, offset 393,829-393,830, mult 1, skip). Per-window bests 1.2816-1.3462: 0.7 1.2848 (Plpri mabinogion lt), 0.8 1.2816 (Plidx agrippa dg), 0.9 1.3003 (Plpri emerson_selfrel lt), 0.11 1.2964 (Plidx mabinogion lt), 0.12 1.2816 (Pwpri agrippa dg), 0.13 1.3352 (Plen agrippa dg), 0.14 1.3158 (Plen agrippa lt), 0.15 1.3462.
- **Matched nulls** (`rand:292:41`, `rand:292:42`: 292 uniform streams of length 20,000, the same 5.7 M stream-offset pairs; same options): null 1 best **1.3191** (183,310,592 decodes, 2,595 s); null 2 best **1.3417** (183,310,592 decodes, 2,045 s).
- Rule 1.40: **no hit**. The real best (1.3462) lies between the two nulls' bests (1.3191, 1.3417) plus 0.005 above the larger: indistinguishable from a null of this size; its whole-window decode is noise (below).
- **`verify.py` on the top 15** (whole-window decode at the hit's offset, beam 64; 20 random offsets of the same stream as the keystream-matched null): **0 of 15 keep IoC >= 1.40**; whole-window IoC 1.045-1.075 (noise) against the same stream's random-offset controls mean 1.041-1.050, max 1.046-1.067. Top hit: 1.346 (first 300) -> 1.056 over 3,316 runes, controls mean 1.041 max 1.046. `results/kscreen/booknum_segments_verify.txt`.

## Verdict
Negative for this family under this scope. The 80 passing streams include the cumulative sums of prime values and rune indices over letters and words, word-length cumulations and per-sentence sums of six cited books, in both spellings. Not excluded: the 36 streams that fail the flat-IoC rule (per-word sums, per-letter primes, word lengths used as a key; if the key were that skewed stream, the cipher would not be flat, which LP2 is), per-line or per-page sums (no line/page structure was built), other numbers derived from the books (Roman numerals, verse numbers, gematria of whole words in other ciphers, Hebrew/Greek values), Agrippa beyond position 400,000 for the two longest families and the KJV (not included here), streams where only some books or passages are used, windows other than the section starts, and any key that is not an additive stream under the skip/coin rule. The Agrippa copy is raw OCR (noisy numbers shift every sum), so a negative there is weak.
