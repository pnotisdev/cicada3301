# R4 repair s13: are LP2's lag-6 T sites repairs? Items 19-23 of `work/laptop_review/11_next_100.md` (2026-10-24)

Nothing in LP2 (sections 0.7-0.15, 12,956 runes) is solved. Keyless: only LP2's own runes (layout and statistics) and simulations. No decryption.
T(c) = 24 - 2c (mod 29), Gematria Primus index order F = 0 ... EA = 28. A "T site" is an in-word pair (j-6, j) with c_j = T(c_(j-6)); LP2 has 80.

## Question
Items 21 (doublets at T sites), 22 (uniformity of the source rune), 23 (position from the word end), 19-20 (the T grid, rune lag 1-12 x 0-4 word gaps, max over 812 affine forms per lag, with a planted gap-crossing control).

Chance nulls on LP2: 2,000 within-word shuffles (word contents permuted) and 300 word-order shuffles within sections (in-word structure kept, gap cells destroyed).

## Does it support "the T sites are repairs"?
- Supports (weakly): item 23 word-final count 32 matches R4 (31.1), not R7 (23.9, 1.7 sd); item 21's single doublet is consistent with the repair rule (0.28 expected) but equally with R0 and depleted chance: no power.
- Contradicts: nothing at the level of a test; item 22 is flat as every model predicts. The excess at distance 1 (28 vs 19.6) is a 1.9 sd surprise for all repair variants.
- Not a repair signature: (5,1) and lag 9. R4 predicts no excess there (+0.5, -0.2); LP2 shows +52.8 and +7.0. They are borderline (post-hoc 0.03), and the planted lag-5 look-back fits (5,1) but fails (5,2) by 2.4 sd.
- Net: items 21-23 are too weak to decide; none discriminates "repair" from "sparse unlinked echo" (neither writes doublets at chance sites differently), as phase 61's detector analysis already found. The grid says the lag-6 in-word cell is the only large cell (z 5.42 vs null max 2-3).

## What this does NOT exclude
Repairs by another source rule; the unlinked echo (`echo-two-tap`); R4 with different slot or null count rules beyond the presets; replicates use uniform plaintext (word lengths only from English): a plaintext-dependent effect, or non-flat LP2 unigrams beyond what the within-word shuffle captures, is not modelled; the (5,1) and lag-9 excesses are neither explained nor excluded (a post-hoc 3% effect after 41 cells); the 500-replicate P values floor at 0.002; the grid expectation uses each stream's own unigrams (a pair-level approximation, z means near 0 and sd near 1.0 in the null sets confirm calibration); one note: the within-word shuffle null has (5,1) z +0.60 (excess +9.4), so a part of the cell is a shuffle-null property.
