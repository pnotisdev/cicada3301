## The question
The laptop review found (`work/log/laptop_review_2026-10-23.md`, lead 1) that the simulations that fitted R4 draw the
null's slot from 0..L, word edges included (`work/phase57_mechanism.py:77`, `work/mech25/insert47.py:106`), while every R4
plant and the mid-word decoder use interior slots only (`work/phase57_pilot.py:41`, `plant_mid.py:47`,
`phase51_midword_channel.md:9`). If the decoder were blind to edge nulls, every R4 negative of phases 57-60 and the queued
`r4lib` would cover only the interior share of R4.

## What changes
- **The laptop review's lead 1 is closed, negative.** The mid arc detects R4 as fitted (slots 0..L) at the window sizes the R4 negatives used (n 150: 45/48, n 300: 24/24 and more). The R4 negatives of phases 57-60 stand as R4 negatives. `r4lib` can run as queued.
- **Margins are smaller than the interior plants suggested.** Edge nulls cost about 2-4 nats each; the median band drops 11-29 nats (n 100-300). At n 100 recall falls to 16/24, so short windows (clause windows, page restarts) need n >= 150 or the union arc.
- **The union arc exists and is gated** (`work/r4fix/s08_*`). It does not change detection on these plants but adds 5-11 nats of margin and picks the right channel; worth using for new R4 runs, especially on short windows and for the spend channel, at 1.15-1.49x the cost.
- **Where the null sits, how it is checked and whether some words carry two cannot be read from LP2's statistics** (s02, s03, s04). Any decoder must stay robust to these choices rather than assume one.
- **Literal F on top of R4 is excluded by LP2's F share** (s06), not by the decoder.
- **Data pitfall found (s02):** without the git-ignored corpora `work/ref/corpora/`, `fam_data.english` silently falls back to Emerson only and phase 57 does not reproduce (r 0.399 instead of 0.448). The shared simulator's test T0 now fails loudly; older scripts still fall back silently.

## Not excluded
Recall below n 150; skip5 and coin channels under R4 as fitted; more than one null per word in the decoder; a null valued by a function (a check rune); key families outside the library and keyword sets; any window not yet scored. Plant counts are 12-48 per cell; a 90% lower bound needs n >= 200.
