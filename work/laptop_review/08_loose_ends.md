Nothing in LP2 (sections 0.7-0.15, 12,956 runes) is solved. This file audits the repository's own record: gaps in STATUS.md / registry.toml, phases 46-60, number mismatches, over-strong claims. Everything below was read from files in the repository; the only computation was ONE tiny keyless script (seconds of CPU, one thread, below-normal priority, kept in the scratch area, not in the repo), reported as "my check" where used. ## 0. Headline findings (read these first)

1. **Model mismatch inside the R4 programme (new, high value, minutes to test).** The simulations that selected R4 place the extra rune at a uniform slot `0..L` of a word, word edges included (`work/mech25/insert47.py:6-7`, `work/phase57_mechanism.py:77`; phase 47 model B). Every R4 decoder and every R4 plant uses INTERIOR slots only (`work/phase57_pilot.py:41` `randrange(1, L)`; `work/chanscreen/v2/step4/plant_mid.py:8,47`; phase 51 log lines 9-11: "INTERIOR cipher position t in 1..Lc-2"). With the fitted model about 41% of the extra runes are at a word edge (my check: mean 2/(L+1) over LP2's multi-rune words = 0.414 with cipher lengths, 0.49 with lengths reduced by one). So the "12/12 plants recovered" (phase 57), the 44/44 keyword plants (phase 59), the 32/32 and 24/24 number-string plants (phase 60), the two library pilots and the queued `r4lib` campaign all test a decoder against plants drawn from the decoder's own narrower model. The power of the mid arc against the model that actually fitted LP2 was never measured. An edge null is keyless like an interior one, so a decoder that treats it as a real rune loses key alignment from there on; with a null in about 45% of words and about 41% of those at an edge, the first misaligning edge null falls about 25-30 runes into a window (rough estimate, not measured).
2. **Every key-family negative is "R4-free" unless it says otherwise.** Only 8 of the 81 excluded/partial registry rows name insertions or R4 in their `gap` field (keywords-r4, clue-review, p60-wordsums, p60-numkeys-r4, p60-cicada-blobs, p60-xor-keys, p60-560-13, p60-audio; counted by script over `docs/registry.toml`). The other 73 (library, multipliers, recurrences, hashes, signs, pdir, chanscreen v2 n300, H5, book keys, seeds) assume the key advances once per ciphertext rune except at repairs. R4 is the best-fit mechanism in the repository's own words, and under it those decoders lose alignment every ~10 runes. FINDINGS.md section 2 lists them as "excluded" without that condition. R4-aware coverage of the library is two windows done (0.7@0 all channels, 0.15@0 n150) plus seven queued (`jobs/queues/r4lib.txt`: hold/spend only, interior nulls only, section starts only).
3. **The word-length premise is weaker than FINDINGS says, because the baselines were Emerson-like and digraph-spelled.** My check (two-sample chi-square, LP2 2,896 words against Cicada's own solved text, 739 words): in digraph runes chi2 42.5 (df 11, p 1.3e-5, 2-rune share 24.1% vs 15.5%, z -5.5); spelled in letters chi2 25.8 (p 0.007, 2-rune share 17.9% vs 15.5%, z -1.6). `results/analysis/wordlen.txt` prints p 7e-28 for the same letter comparison because it treats the 739-word histogram as exact. The +1-rune-in-42-45% fit (and with it R4's second rule and all mid/edge-arc budgets of about 260 CPU-h) rests on this. The honest statement is "not like Emerson; against Cicada's own text in letters a residual p of about 0.007 on a small sample".
4. **Results exist that no registry row, STATUS line or TRIED line records.** The night2 H5 keyword x flat-library sweeps (`results/h5rs_flat_summary.txt`, `results/h5rs_nonflat_summary.txt`: 0 candidates, best real cell 1.5167, L 2..13) and the night2 layer2 slices (`results/layer2/bigram_solved*.tsv`, `bigram_words*`, `periodic_core*`, `bigram_combined*`) are only mentioned in `docs/QUEUE.md:54-60`. STATUS "Left to do" #7 (layer2 slices) and #9 (H1: "the remaining action is RUNNING the sweep", registry.toml:962-970) are stale: they have run (L 2..13 only; L 14..20, litf=1 and offsets 1950-2000 did not).

### A. Does the mid arc see R4 as fitted? (edge-slot plants)  [leads 1-2]

- **Decoder**: the unchanged mid arc (`work/chanscreen/v2/backward_mid.rs`, `step4/lp_pilot_mid.py`, nu 0.42), search the TRUE stream only at every offset, both signs, hold and spend (4 channels for a second pass).
- **Metrics**: detected = true cell S_guard >= T300 = 29.8; true cell within 3 nats of the band best; score vs the number of edge nulls and the position of the first edge null.
- **Controls**: positive = interior mode (expect 12/12, band minimum 57.9 / 119.5 as in phase 57; any other result means the harness is broken); null = matched iid-key plant per mode in the same stream (expect max near -18 at n150 and -56 at n300).
- **Decision**: detection in the 0..L mode below about 90% means the r4lib negatives and the phase 57-60 R4 negatives cover only the interior share; then build a union arc (null at any cipher position 0..Lc-1 of a word, weight nu/Lc; extend the S0/S1/S2 state machine of phase 51 with the word-start and word-end cases), gate it against the dense reference as phase 51 did (G1, G2, G3), and rerun the two library pilots and the queued windows. ### B. Rebuild the word-length baseline from Cicada's own text  [lead 3]
- **Data**: LP1 solved plaintext words (739 words, 2,977 runes) from `lp.KNOWN`, in runes (digraph) and in letters; Emerson and Walden as the comparison corpora; LP2's 2,896 word lengths.
- **Tests (keyless)**: (i) fit q of "+1 rune in a share q of multi-rune words" against each baseline by minimum G, with a parametric bootstrap that includes the baseline's own sampling noise (resample N_model words, compare to LP2's resampled histogram), report q, CI and p; (ii) the same with the extra rune allowed in one-rune words, to settle the 83 vs 50.6 / 87 accounting; (iii) a null model of "no insertion, letter spelling" with a genre mixture (solved text register) and its p; (iv) split the 2-rune bin into function words by their solved-text frequency to see whether the deficit sits in the words English uses most.

## 6. Killed by known signal

- **Plaintext autokey at any lag (G11, d 9-400, a != +/-1)**: the ciphertext of c = p + a*p(-d) is the sum of two English-shaped streams, unigram excess about 0.02 in the x29 IoC scale (the repository measured 1.039-1.057 for the tested lags, `PLAN.md:146`), against LP2's 1.002 +/- 0.0005. Excluded by flatness before any lag scan.
- **Scan-derived or other structured byte streams as additive keys (G10)** unless their value histogram over the window is flat to about 0.2%: ciphertext excess is the product of the plaintext's and the key's spectra, so a key histogram off uniform by that much already breaks LP2's flat unigrams. A one-second histogram pre-filter should precede any screen (the library's 858 non-flat streams are handled that way: only L <= 8 H5, `results/h5rs_nonflat_summary.txt`).
- **Base-10 digit streams read directly (RAND digits, `open-sources`)**: key values 0-9 only; flatness fails (the registry already says "per-word sums and base-10 digits fail flatness").
- **Edge-only insertion** (phase 47 C, joint p 0.000) and **single-rune doublet ciphers** (the gap-doublet test, 23 of 86): unchanged. Note: lead 1 does not revive edge-only insertion; it asks for edge slots in addition to interior ones.
