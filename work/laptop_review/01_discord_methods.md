## Killed by known signal

- **avaster's "circumference" cipher (22/10/2026, #general-solving, 00:09-00:40).** The rule is cumulative: each rune is the previous rune plus a prime-dependent step times (plaintext minus NG, value 21). Two equal ciphertext runes then occur only where the plaintext is NG. Killed on two counts. (a) Doublets across word gaps need NG at word starts; a screenshot posted in #general-solving on 22/10/2026 at about 00:30-00:34 gives 2 of 2,977 word-initial NG in the solved pages against 23 of 86 LP2 doublets across word gaps (the corrected figure, `discord-cumulative`; the post used 26). Verify on the repo transcription before citing. (b) The same post's direct test, with a planted solved page at start point 1,234 recovered, puts LP2 best at 1.236 (prime stream) and 1.181 (prime-1 stream), below the 1.40 line that real plaintext reaches. This is third-party, unverified, and a cumulative prime cipher was already excluded as "avaster's cumulative prime cipher" (`TRIED` 2026-10-22 [discord-cumulative]). Step-domain and ciphertext-feedback variants are in `TRIED` (step28, chain).
- **Atbash and Caesar plus or minus 1 (Zaveon Carlio 02/10/2026; Maedan 22/10/2026).** Any fixed one-to-one letter map keeps equal runes equal, so it cannot create or remove doublets. The 0.66% doublet rate (`obs-doublets`) and the flat unigrams (`obs-flat`) rule out a monoalphabetic layer.
- **"Next letters depend on the previous ciphered output" (avaster 22/10/2026 00:20).** Ciphertext feedback is tested (`TRIED` 2026-10-17 "Ciphertext feedback ... [chain]", best 1.07).
