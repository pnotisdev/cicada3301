## A. Repair the R4 programme first (the decoder may not see R4 as fitted)

1. **Edge-slot R4 plants against the unchanged mid arc** [1]. This decides whether every R4 negative so far covers R4 or only its interior share. 20-50 CPU-min. High.
2. **Run `insert47.py` with interior-only slots** [2]. Learn whether LP2 prefers slots 0..L or 1..L-1 (edge-pair share 0.48, doublet-word length 6.08). 10-20 CPU-min. Med.
3. **Build a union arc** (null at any cipher position, weight nu/Lc) if idea 1 shows lost power. Gate it against the dense reference (G1-G3) as phase 51 did. 1-2 days. Med.
4. **Reorder the queued `r4lib` behind ideas 1-3** (a desktop decision). This avoids spending about 20 CPU-h on a possibly blind decoder. 0 CPU. High value.
5. **Rerun the two R4 library pilots (0.7@0, 0.15@0) with the union arc** once it passes its gates. About 3 CPU-h. Low-med.
6. **Allow 0, 1 or 2 nulls per word.** The fit assumes at most one; test a two-null share against the word-length G and the doublet split. Keyless, minutes. Low-med.
7. **Check whether the null is compared with both neighbours** (R4: left only). Simulate both rules; compare the in-word doublet position profile. Keyless, minutes. Low.
8. **Power map of the mid arc** over window length n (100-600) and a mis-specified nu (0.30-0.55) and q (0.70-0.95). It tells us how far a wrong guess degrades recall. 1-2 CPU-h. Med (calibration).
9. **Plant Cicada-style plaintext under R4** (solved-page register, literal F skips, Cicada spellings) instead of generic English. Recall may differ. 30 CPU-min. Low-med.
10. **R4 x the 28 key multipliers** for the library. The `multipliers` row is R4-free. Cost scales x28; pilot one window first. Low.
11. **R4 on stride windows**, not only section starts, for the library. Restart-free keys are seen anywhere, but a key starting mid-section is not. CPU-hours per window. Low.
12. **R4 coin and skip5 channels** for keywords, number strings and the MB files. Only hold/spend ran. About 3x those runs. Low.
13. **R4 x page and clause restarts** with about 60 Cicada-named streams [5]. 2-5 CPU-h. Low-med.
14. **One shared R4 reference simulator** used by every plant generator and simulation, with a unit test that the plant slot distribution matches the fitted model. It prevents a repeat of lead 1. Half a day. High (process).
15. **A cost/benefit table for a full R4 library pass** after ideas 1-3: expected recall gain per CPU-hour, so the 260 CPU-h question is decided by numbers. 1 h. Med.
